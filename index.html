Ministry Gazette — Issue No. 001 Bangkok · 2024

One in three adults is single.
None of the world was built for them.

"We don't fix single people. We serve them — as they are."

For decades, the world assumed singlehood was temporary. A problem to solve. A phase before the real story begins. Every product, every city, every policy was designed for the couple, the family — the plural.

Ministry of Single exists to correct that assumption — and to build, for the first time, a genuine institution that places the single person at the center.

  • I Singlehood is not a deficit. It is a chapter — sometimes the longest, most defining chapter of a human life.
  • II Understanding before building. We ask what singles actually want before we offer them anything.
  • III Community without agenda. We connect singles who want to be connected — no matchmaking, no expectation to change.
  • IV Value first, revenue second. We earn from delivering genuine service — not from burning cash to buy attention.
  • V The single person is the hero. Not the audience. Not the subject. The center of everything we build.

Six things we
do for single people.

Not assumptions about what singles need. These come directly from listening to thousands of single people across Thailand and Southeast Asia.

01 A Voice & A Platform Singles have been statistically invisible. We make them visible — through research, media, and public advocacy. When brands, cities, and policymakers design for singles, it's because we put the data in front of them.
02 Real Research on Single Life Through WhatSingleWant.com, we run the only dedicated research platform tracking what single people actually want. Not assumptions. Not stereotypes. Data — collected, analyzed, and published.
03 Community Without Pressure We connect singles who want to be connected. No matchmaking agenda. No pressure to change. Genuine community for people living single lives — on their own terms.
04 The Single Expo Southeast Asia's first lifestyle expo built entirely around the single person. Not a dating event. A celebration — brands that serve singles, speakers who live it, experiences designed for one.
05 Content That Actually Sees You Stories, guides, and insights that reflect the actual reality of single life — not the stereotype. Solo travel, personal finance, housing, career, wellness. Written by people living it.
06 A Bridge to Brands That Care We connect single-friendly brands with the audience they've been ignoring. When a brand partners with Ministry of Single, they're not buying impressions — they're earning genuine trust with a deeply underserved market.
